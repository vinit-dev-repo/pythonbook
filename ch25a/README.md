## What this chapter teaches

- How to parse a small CSV policy into date/`Decimal` observation records
- Why a complete candidate tuple must exist before public series data changes
- How record number, field, reason, and original conversion cause clarify a bad input
- How a tuple protects its outer slots without making retained record objects deeply immutable
