## What this chapter teaches

- What a character really is, and the difference between a **code point** and a **byte**
- The `bytes` and `bytearray` types, and byte literals like `b"..."`
- How `.encode()` and `.decode()` turn text into bytes and bytes back into text
- The encodings you will meet most: UTF-8, UTF-16, Latin-1, cp1252 and ASCII
