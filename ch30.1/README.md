## What this chapter teaches

- Say why repeated unexplained numbers and strings can cause bugs.
- Define an **enumeration**, or enum, with `class ... (Enum)`.
- Say what **singleton** means for an enum member, and why a plain member is not equal to its raw value.
- Find an enum member by its name or by its value.
