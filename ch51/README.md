## What this chapter teaches

- Why a picture can show you things a table of numbers hides
- The two ways to use matplotlib, and why the "Figure and Axes" way is the habit to build
- How to draw and style a single chart, and how to draw a grid of several charts at once
- The core chart types — line, bar, histogram, scatter, pie, box — and when to use each
- How to plot straight from a pandas DataFrame
- What seaborn adds on top of matplotlib, and its statistical chart types
